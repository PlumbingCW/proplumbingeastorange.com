East Orange ZIP Codes We Cover

Inside the city we cover ZIP codes 07017, 07018 and 07019, from the Central Avenue and Main Street corridors to the neighborhoods near Brick Church and the Garden State Parkway. Our drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection crews are the ones most often called across these ZIPs.

East Orange runs its own water system. The Water Commission at 99 S Grove Street draws from wells in the Passaic aquifer, and the city's hard water page describes the water as very hard. Homeowners and landlords here own the sewer lateral from the building to the commission main and the water line from the building to the curb stop. That split matters when a crew is deciding who to call about a street side problem.

How Each Neighboring Town Differs

Orange sits directly west of East Orange and shares much of its housing: about three quarters of homes are rented and most are in multi-unit buildings, according to the Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 estimates. Orange runs its own water department and permits plumbing work through its Building and Construction Division.

Newark wraps around East Orange to the east and south and has its own water and sewer utility. We serve the western and northern wards closest to us, not the whole city. Bloomfield lies to the north and has an active lead service line replacement program that often triggers homeowner side plumbing work.

West Orange is a hillier, more owner occupied township with older homes and New Jersey American Water mains that the utility has been replacing. Irvington is a dense, mostly rented township south of Newark where multi-family buildings dominate. Neither borders East Orange, so we do not call them neighbors, but both are a short drive from Central Avenue.

What Is The Same In Every Town

Wherever the address is, the call works the same way. A person answers, asks what the water is doing and tells you what to shut off. A crew comes with tools for the likely cause, confirms it and gives you a price before work starts.

  • One 24/7 phone number for every address.

  • Written notes of what we found for landlords and managers.
