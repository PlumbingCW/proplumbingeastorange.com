What We Find In Orange: Old Walk-Ups And Two-Family Houses

What We Find In Orange: Cold Snaps, Frozen Lines And Cold Apartments

Exterior walls, unheated stairwells and basement window wells are where pipes freeze first, and a frozen line often announces itself as one faucet with no water before it splits.

A split pipe that thaws is the moment a quiet problem becomes a flood, often at night. Close the main before you try to thaw anything, then call. Before We Arrive If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

These four steps limit the damage while an emergency plumber is on the way. In a building with several apartments, two of them involve other people, so start those conversations early.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the valve clockwise until it stops. In a multi-unit building the shutoff is often in a shared basement, so ask the landlord or superintendent at once. If it will not turn, do not force it. Tell us.

Kill power near the water

If water is near outlets, a light fixture or the panel, switch off that circuit at the panel only if the floor around it is dry. If the panel itself is wet, stay back and call 911.

Clear the area and tell the building

Move rugs, boxes and electronics off wet floors. Tenants should notify the landlord now and photograph the damage, and should warn neighbors below. A dated record helps with repairs and insurance.
