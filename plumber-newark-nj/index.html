Before We Arrive If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock. These steps limit damage until the plumber arrives, and several of them involve a landlord because three quarters of Newark homes are rented.

Find and close the main

In a rental building the main is often in a basement the tenant cannot enter. Call the superintendent or landlord now. If you own the building, turn the valve clockwise and tell us if it sticks.

Cut power in wet rooms

Switch off breakers for any room with water on the ceiling or near outlets, but only if you can reach the panel on dry floor. If the panel is wet, step away and call 911.

Move belongings and warn the building

Lift boxes and electronics off the floor. Knock on the doors below you, because leaks travel. Photograph the damage with the date so the owner and insurer have a record.

Call our 24/7 line

Call (973) 488-5410 with your ZIP, the floor and what the water is doing. An emergency plumber is dispatched and the price is explained before work starts.
