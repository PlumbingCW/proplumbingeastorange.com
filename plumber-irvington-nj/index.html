Basement Backup Or Sewer Overflow

The most common emergency in a multi-unit building is a drain that fails below the lowest fixture. Water comes up through the basement floor drain or fills a first-floor tub when an upstairs unit runs the washer. That pattern points to the shared line, not to a single sink, and it affects everyone above it.

We start with drain cleaning to restore flow, then decide if the line needs a camera inspection to find the cause. A cable can open the line, but a collapsed or offset section will clog again. We tell the owner what we saw so the next step is based on the pipe, not on a guess.

No Heat Or No Hot Water In A Multifamily Building

A failed boiler or central water heater in a building with many apartments is an emergency because dozens of people lose service together. The state heat rule, N.J.A.C. 5:10-14.4, sets the heat season from October 1 to May 15, with minimums of 68 degrees F by day and 65 degrees F at night. The Irvington building page also lists a municipal emergency heat hotline at 973-399-6666, which tenants can use alongside a call to their landlord.

For owners and managers, we handle boiler repair and central hot water failures at any hour. We look at the controls, the circulator, the relief valve and the pressure before we decide if a repair or replacement is the right answer, and we describe both options in plain terms. Our commercial plumbing team also works on the larger systems in apartment buildings.

Burst Pipe Or Active Leak: Before We Arrive

Irvington's two-family and three-to-four family houses stack kitchens and bathrooms directly above each other, so a burst pipe on the second floor can run through two units. The first ten minutes decide how much damage follows.

Shut off the main valve

In a two or three family house the main is often on the basement wall where the line enters. If separate valves serve each unit, close the one for the unit that is leaking. Tell the people below.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is dripping through a ceiling light or near outlets, turn off that circuit from a dry spot. Do not touch a panel while standing in water.

Clear the immediate area

Move furniture, rugs and electronics out from under the drip. Use pots and buckets, and open a faucet on a lower floor once the main is off to drain the line.

Call our 24/7 emergency line

Give us the address, which floor is leaking and whether the neighbors below are affected. We send a technician at any hour.

Once the water is stopped, burst pipe repair replaces the failed section and checks the pipe on either side. In a building built around 1960, which is the median year for Irvington housing, the pipes next to a break are often in the same condition as the one that failed, so we say so and let the owner decide.

Low Water Pressure Or A Stuck Valve In An Older Building

Not every emergency is a flood. Sometimes one apartment loses pressure while the rest of the building is fine, or a shutoff valve that has not moved in decades will not close when it is needed. A seized valve is a real risk in a building of this age, because it turns a small leak into a larger one.

We test the valves during an emergency visit and tell the owner which ones should be replaced while the line is open. That small step makes the next burst far easier to stop, and it is worth doing before the cold season starts.

Water Heater Leaking Or Failed

A leaking tank in a basement shared by several tenants is both a plumbing and a landlord problem. We shut the supply, drain the tank and tell you whether water heater repair is possible or if the tank has reached the end of its life. Many of these calls are replacements, and we explain the sizing in terms of how many apartments the unit serves.
