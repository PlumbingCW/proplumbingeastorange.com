A dripping faucet seems minor until you look at the water bill, and a failed supply valve is an emergency the second it lets go. We repair faucets, shower valves, tub spouts, angle stops and outdoor spigots in East Orange at any hour. We find out why the part failed before we replace it.

Water quality matters here. The city's own page describes East Orange water as very hard, and mineral scale is behind a large share of worn cartridges, stuck handles and clogged aerators. A repair that ignores the scale tends to come back.

Water Spraying From A Valve Or Pipe? Do This First

A burst supply line or a broken shutoff under a sink can empty a lot of water into a cabinet quickly. Acting in the first minute limits what gets wet.

Close the fixture shutoff

Look under the sink or behind the toilet for a small oval or round handle. Turn it clockwise until the flow stops.

If that fails, close the main

The building or unit main shutoff stops the whole line. In an apartment building, call the super or manager for the riser valve.

Open a low faucet

After the water is off, open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the line and ease pressure on the failed part.

Clear the cabinet and floor

Empty the cabinet, soak up water and keep it from reaching outlets. Then call us with the location and what you saw.

Dripping And Leaking Faucets

Most drips come from the part inside the handle. Older compression faucets use a rubber washer that presses on a metal seat, and both wear down. Newer single handle faucets use a cartridge, and hard water scale on the cartridge's seals lets water creep past.

A leak at the base of the spout points to worn o-rings. A leak under the handle points to a packing nut or cartridge. We identify the type, replace the failing part and test the faucet at full and partial flow so the repair holds.

Shower Valves, Showerheads And Tub Faucets

A shower that drips after it is turned off has a worn cartridge or valve seat. Water that runs hot and cold at random, or a handle that is hard to turn, often means scale or a worn pressure balancing cartridge. A dripping tub spout that still sends water to the showerhead has a failed diverter.

Showerheads clog when scale builds in the small nozzles. Soaking in vinegar clears mild cases, and we replace the head when the nozzles are plugged for good. If the valve sits behind tile, we check the access panel from the other side of the wall before suggesting any tile work.

Burst And Failing Supply Valves And Angle Stops

The small valve under a sink or behind a toilet is an angle stop, and in older buildings it may not have been turned in decades. When it finally gets moved, the stem or packing can fail and water sprays. Some stops will not close fully at all, which makes every later repair harder.

We replace failed angle stops and supply lines so the next faucet job has a working shutoff. If a valve will not close and water is running, shut the main and call right away. Replacing faucets and valves is ordinary maintenance under N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7, though changing the pipe layout is not, so we tell you which side of that line a job is on.

Outdoor Hose Bibs, Spigots And Freeze Damage

An outdoor spigot is a common place for a winter failure. Northern New Jersey uses a frost depth of 36 inches, and Newark's normals show 79.9 days a year at or below 32 degrees, with a January mean low of 25.5 degrees. A hose left connected holds water in the spigot, which freezes and splits the pipe behind the wall.

The damage often shows up in spring, when the spigot is turned on and water floods the wall cavity. Disconnect hoses in autumn, and close and drain any interior shutoff to the spigot. Replacing a hose bib valve in a single family dwelling is listed as ordinary maintenance in N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7, and burst piping behind the wall is covered on our burst pipe repair page.

Hard Water Scale In Aerators And Cartridges

If water pressure drops at one faucet but not others, the aerator is probably clogged. Unscrew it, soak it in vinegar and rinse it, and flow often returns. If the faucet still sputters, scale has reached the cartridge or the supply line.

Scale shortens the life of every moving part in a faucet, so we mention it when we replace a worn cartridge. We cannot soften water at the fixture, but we can pick parts that tolerate it and clean the seats before reassembly.

A repair makes sense when the faucet body is sound and parts are available. Replacement makes sense when the finish is gone, the body is cracked or the model is obsolete. The table lays out how we weigh the two.

Kitchen, Bathroom And Laundry Fixtures

Kitchen faucets work harder than any other fixture. A pull-down sprayer can crack its hose, a loose spout can leak from the base, and a diverter that sticks sends water out the side instead of the spray head. We repair these parts or replace the faucet when the finish or body is past saving.

Bathroom vanity faucets are usually simpler, but the supply lines and pop-up drains beside them often leak first. Laundry sinks and utility sinks use basic compression faucets that wear fast and are easy to fix. If a washing machine valve behind the wall leaks or drips, close it and let us know, because that valve is often the oldest one in the building.

Signs A Fixture Is Wasting Water

A faucet dripping once a second can waste thousands of gallons over a year, and a tub spout that leaks into the drain hides the loss completely. Check the meter or the water bill if usage has climbed with no change in the household. A stain on the cabinet floor, a soft vanity bottom or a musty smell under a sink all point to a slow leak.

Handles that need more turning to shut off, spray that splits sideways and a hiss behind the wall are early warnings. Fixing a small leak is far cheaper than replacing a vanity or ceiling that it ruins. Call us when the signs appear and we will check the fixture and its supply lines together.

Faucet And Fixture Repair In Rentals

With 69 percent of occupied homes renter occupied, many faucet calls begin as a tenant's report. A leaking faucet that goes unreported can stain a cabinet and the ceiling below it, so tell the landlord early and keep a record. We can also coordinate with property managers who need a written note on what was found.

If a unit has several fixtures with the same problem, such as scaled aerators in every bathroom, a managed visit that covers them together is often more efficient. Call and tell us how many fixtures are involved. Our leak detection service helps when water is appearing and you cannot see which fixture it comes from.
